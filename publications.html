QuantStream3D: Geometry-Aligned Low-Bit KV Cache Quantization for Streaming Visual Geometry Transformers

Zunhai Su*, Keyu Fan*, Ruishan Guo, Zhongzhu Zhou, Jingzehua Xu, Bohan Sun, Hansen Feng, Ruihan Hu, Yik-Chung Wu, Ngai Wong

In streaming 3D reconstruction, quantization errors in the KV cache can propagate from one view to the next. QuantStream3D assigns higher precision to geometrically sensitive entries and distills the student using the quantized history it generates. With most cache entries stored at 2 bits, the evaluated models retain nearly the same geometric accuracy in most settings.

Efficient AI

My long-term research goal is to make advanced AI run reliably and efficiently on everyday devices and in large-scale services. By lowering barriers to deployment and use, I hope to bring AI into homes everywhere and transform the way we live. This vision guides my research agenda for LLMs and other AI models, which spans four connected themes.

Research Areas

Efficient Model Design & Architecture

I aim to explore modeling paradigms and architectures that can expand model capabilities at lower computational cost. A central question is how to organize and allocate computation to match task demands while staying efficient as models and workloads grow.

Efficient Model Training

I want to understand how training resources translate into model capabilities. I’m interested in where additional training effort makes the greatest difference and how to maintain training stability and model quality with fewer resources.

Efficient Edge & Cloud Inference

I want to understand when gains in model efficiency translate into better performance on real systems. On devices, memory and latency shape what is practical. In the cloud, throughput and serving cost shape what can scale. These constraints guide how I think about model design, software, and hardware together.

Model Understanding & Mechanistic Interpretability

Across design, training, and inference

I want to use an understanding of model mechanisms to guide decisions about efficiency. That means developing testable explanations of which computations a capability depends on and what happens when those computations change.
